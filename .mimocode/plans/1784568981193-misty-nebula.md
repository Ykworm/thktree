| 分组 | 键前缀 | 示例键 | 文件来源 |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| 备份恢复 | `backup*` | `backupDeleteTitle`, `backupRestoreSuccess` | `backup_restore_screen.dart` |

| 图片 | `image*` | `imageSourceSelect`, `imageFromCamera` | `chat_screen.dart` |
| 搜索 | `search*` | `searchRecent`, `searchClearAll` | `search_content.dart` |
| 设置 | `settings*` | `settingsBackupRestore`, `settingsBreadcrumb` | `settings_screen.dart` |
